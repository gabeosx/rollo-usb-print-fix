Rollo USB Print Fix

Install the printing fix for your USB-connected Rollo. Keep using your existing printer and the official Rollo driver.

Before you begin

  • Install the official Rollo USB driver and add your printer in System Settings → Printers & Scanners.
  • Finish or cancel pending jobs in Print Center.
  • Have an administrator account available to authorize installation.

The fix applies to all compatible USB Rollo printers on this Mac, including renamed printers. Wi-Fi and AirPrint printers are not changed.

Labels may take about five seconds longer to print. A removal tool is included.
