Ready to print

  1. Choose your existing Rollo printer in your usual app.
  2. For a standard shipping label, select 4×6 paper, portrait and 100% scale.
  3. Use Default Settings, or a saved preset that uses 203 dpi.
  4. Print one label to check the result.

If the printer was paused before setup, resume it in Print Center when you are ready.

To remove the fix later, open the setup app and choose Remove….
